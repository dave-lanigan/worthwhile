# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred from the existing product: individuals privately maintaining a household financial plan and checking whether their long-term choices remain viable.

## Product Purpose

Worthwhile turns a person's current income, assets, expenses, liabilities, and savings allocations into an understandable long-term net-worth forecast. Success is a user understanding their financial direction and confidently adjusting a plan when it does not work.

## Positioning

A private, editable financial forecast where deterministic and Monte Carlo views expose both a projected path and the uncertainty around it.

## Operating Context

Users revisit a personal plan as finances change, inspect a selected future date, and add or revise ledger entries. Signed-in users save separate financial profiles; guests can explore a temporary forecast.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Forecasts use USD and household net worth. The tool includes deterministic projections, Monte Carlo analysis, risk assumptions, financial ledgers, saved profiles, and U.S. household wealth-percentile context. Preserve the existing calculation behavior, terminology, privacy boundaries, sign-in behavior, and saved-plan behavior.

## Brand Commitments

The tool is private and personal, not advisor- or sales-oriented. Current functionality must remain immediately usable for signed-in and guest visitors.

## Evidence on Hand

The application supplies real editable financial data and projections. Do not invent financial outcomes, customers, benchmarks, or personal records.

## Product Principles

- Make long-term financial direction understandable at a glance.
- Let meaningful adjustments remain deliberate and traceable.
- Treat uncertainty plainly rather than hiding it behind certainty theater.
- Keep personal finance data and usage private.
